# Markly Africa — SEO Keyword → Page Map (v1)

A working map of *which keyword lives on which page*, so content and metadata target
intent deliberately instead of scattering ~150 keywords everywhere. Built from the
client keyword brief, filtered through what Markly can honestly claim and where it
actually operates (Lagos, Nigeria; Africa + international).

---

## Guiding principles

1. **Nigeria-first, Africa-second.** Real office + real jurisdiction knowledge = the
   terms we can rank for and mean. Broad "…Africa" head terms are a 12–18-month play.
2. **One primary keyword per page**, plus 2–4 secondary. Cramming all 150 onto a few
   pages reads as spam and dilutes relevance.
3. **Long-tail is the near-term win** — lower competition, higher intent, and truthful
   to Nigeria expertise ("how to comply with the NDPA", "register a trademark in Nigeria").
4. **No `<meta name="keywords">`** — Google has ignored it since ~2009. Keywords live in
   titles, H1/H2s, body copy, and structured data.
5. **No per-country landing pages** (Ghana/Kenya/SA/Rwanda) until there's real presence
   or a named local partner — thin/doorway pages don't rank and hurt trust in a YMYL field.
6. **Don't claim "lawyers"** unless Markly is a licensed law firm — use "advisory",
   "consultants", "professional services".

## Part 5 — What NOT to do (explicitly out of scope)

- ❌ **Per-country landing pages** (Ghana / Kenya / South Africa / Rwanda) — no presence =
  thin, doorway-like, unrankable, and a trust risk. Revisit only with real market presence.
- ❌ **Keyword stuffing** — one primary + a few secondary per page. More ≠ better.
- ❌ **"IP lawyers Nigeria"** and similar — implies licensed legal practice. Use "advisory".
- ❌ **Broad "…Africa" head terms as a near-term target** — legitimate long-game, but don't
  measure success against them in the first few months.

## How to measure (for the client)

- **Implementation (now):** Rich Results Test (structured data valid), URL Inspection
  ("indexed"), Lighthouse SEO score — all Google's own tools.
- **Results (over months):** Search Console → **Performance** report. Track impressions,
  clicks, average position, and — most importantly — the **real queries** Markly appears
  for. That's the only true keyword data, and it tells us which of the above to double down on.
